About Cloudside

A proper shop, not just a website

We're a vape, hookah and tobacco shop on Atlas Road in Boksburg. You can walk in, and you can also order from anywhere in the country.

Who we are

Atlas Junction, Boksburg

Cloudside Vapor is at 0A Edel Road in Atlas Junction. Walk in and you'll see hookah pipes along the top shelf, a whole wall of disposables, and rows of 100ml and 120ml juices from local and international brands.

We keep a wide range because people vape differently. Some want a disposable they can grab and go. Others are building a tank setup, and plenty just want their usual shisha flavour and a box of coals for the weekend.

Everything on the shelf is also on this website, and we deliver anywhere in South Africa. If you're not sure what to get, message us. You'll talk to someone who works in the shop.

A few house rules

Strictly 18+

We sell nicotine, so we ask for ID in the shop, on collection and on delivery. No exceptions, even if we know you.

Only real stock online

If something sells out, it comes off the website. You won't order something and then wait a week to hear it's gone.

Ask before you buy

Not sure a pod fits your device, or what nicotine strength to start on? Send us a photo or a question on WhatsApp first.
